First team & Under 23s Overview

Our Men's First Team plays in the Southern Conference competition, alongside the Under-23 development team. This is the highest level available locally and provides a great opportunity for Rotorua players to compete. The squads are coached by head coach Marvin Koeppen (UEFA B-Licensed), with Brendan Squibb taking over the U23 team and George Oake as assistant coach.

Bay teams overview

Bay League Teams For those who enjoy playing but are not seeking high-level competition, we have two teams in the local Bay leagues, including an over-35 team. The Bay teams provide opportunities to play regardless of age, skill level, or fitness.
