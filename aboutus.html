WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Ngongotahā AFC

Ngongotahā Association Football Club is a community-focused football club based in Rotorua, New Zealand. Since 1966, our club has provided opportunities for people to enjoy football, build friendships and become part of something bigger than the game.

We believe football should be accessible to everyone. From children kicking a ball for the first time to experienced players competing at senior level, our club brings people together through a shared love of the game.

Our identity is built around our people: our players, coaches, volunteers, supporters, sponsors and whānau. Their time, commitment and passion help make Ngongotahā AFC what it is today.

OUR STORY

A Club Built Across Generations

Every football club has a story. Ours has been shaped by decades of players, coaches, volunteers and families who have worn the orange and black and helped build the Ngongotahā AFC community.

From local football beginnings to memorable championship campaigns and cup runs, the club has continued to evolve while remaining connected to its community roots.

1966

The Beginning

Ngongotahā Football Club was established, beginning a journey that would see generations of local players and families become part of the club.

THROUGH THE DECADES

Growing Through Football

The club developed through regional competitions, building its reputation through the dedication of players, coaches and volunteers. Ngongotahā also competed in the Northern League era, including at second-division level.

2011

Federation Championship Success

A memorable season brought the club a Federation Championship title and promotion, marking an important achievement in its competitive history.

2014

A Memorable Chatham Cup Run

Ngongotahā reached the round of 16 in the Chatham Cup, creating another significant chapter in the club's story.

2016

An Unbeaten Premiership Campaign

The club claimed its first Waikato-Bay of Plenty Federation Premiership title after completing an unbeaten championship campaign.

2026

Sixty Years and Looking Forward

As Ngongotahā AFC celebrates 60 years, we honour the people and achievements that shaped our past while continuing to develop football opportunities for future generations.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Club Values

Football is about more than the result on the scoreboard. The way we treat people, support one another and represent our community matters just as much as what happens on the pitch.

01 / COMMUNITY

A Place to Belong

We bring people together through football. Players, whānau, volunteers and supporters all contribute to a club where people can build friendships and feel welcome.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Better Every Day

We want players to improve their skills, confidence and understanding of the game. We value learning, hard work and creating opportunities for players to progress.

03 / INCLUSION

Football for Everyone

We want football to be an opportunity for people of different ages, abilities and backgrounds to participate and enjoy being part of a team.

04 / TEAMWORK

Together We Achieve

Football relies on people working together. We encourage respect, communication, commitment and support for teammates both on and off the pitch.

05 / CLUB PRIDE

Proud to Wear Orange

We take pride in representing Ngongotahā, respecting our opponents and officials, and contributing positively to the reputation of our club.

06 / THE FUTURE

Building What's Next

We want to keep developing the club, strengthening our pathways and creating positive football experiences for the next generation.

BEYOND THE PITCH

Football Is About People

A football club cannot run on players alone. Behind every training session, match day and community event are people giving their time, energy and support.

Our volunteers help keep the club moving. Coaches support player development. Families encourage their tamariki. Sponsors help make opportunities possible. Supporters bring atmosphere and pride to the sidelines.

We also believe football can make a positive difference beyond organised competition. Community initiatives, accessible football programmes and opportunities for people to reconnect with the game all help strengthen the relationship between our club and Rotorua.

Whether someone has played for decades or has never kicked a ball before, we want Ngongotahā AFC to remain a club that people can connect with and be proud of.
