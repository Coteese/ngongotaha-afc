The CR1 Project

Supporting Children in Rotorua

Children in Rotorua face numerous challenges, including poverty, limited access to education, and fewer opportunities for personal development. Football can play a vital role in helping children build confidence, teamwork skills, friendships and a sense of belonging.

Ngongotahā AFC's CR1 Project addresses these challenges by providing a free football programme for children from low-income families. Through football, we aim to support physical, mental and educational development, giving every child an opportunity to get involved, have fun and thrive.

Community

A Community Effort

Together with our community, we aim to create a brighter future for Rotorua's children. The CR1 programme gives young people the opportunity to get involved in football while learning valuable life skills that can help set them up for a positive future.

We are extremely grateful to Aaron Lawrence and Trevor Johnston for helping to get this programme off the ground, and to the New Zealand Community Trust (NZCT) for their generous grant of $21,060 to support the project.
