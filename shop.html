Important Information

Kit Hire & Purchase

Playing kit can be hired or purchased, depending on the item. Socks are purchase-only.

Playing Shirts

Players can choose to hire a playing shirt for the season or purchase one, subject to club availability and the applicable club policy.

  • Choose the required size.
  • Select hire or purchase where offered.
  • Hired shirts must be returned according to club instructions.
  • Purchased shirts belong to the customer.

Playing Shorts

Playing shorts can also be hired or purchased. Select the appropriate size and option when adding the item to your bag.

  • Available options are shown for each product.
  • Hire availability will need to be confirmed by the club.
  • Hired items must be returned as instructed.
  • Purchase prices and hire terms will be confirmed before launch.

Club Socks

Club socks must be purchased. They are not available for hire because socks can wear out or break during the season.

  • Purchase-only item.
  • Choose the available size.
  • Additional pairs can be purchased when needed.
  • Stock and sizing will be confirmed by the club.

Hoodies & Merchandise

Club hoodies and supporter merchandise are intended for purchase. These items are not part of the playing kit hire arrangement.

  • Choose from the sizes offered.
  • Product photos will be added as they become available.
  • Limited releases and pre-orders could be introduced later.
  • Final product details will be confirmed before launch.
PLEASE NOTE: Shirts and shorts may be hired or purchased where available. Socks must always be purchased and cannot be hired. All prices and hire arrangements on this demonstration page are examples only and must be confirmed by Ngongotāhā AFC.
