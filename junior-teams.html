Ngongotahā AFC

Junior Football

Youth Registrations & Development

Junior football at Ngongotahā is about giving kids a place to play, improve and make friends. From our youngest players through to Senior football, our teams train locally, compete across Rotorua and form the foundation of the club's future.

Our Development Focus

We are focused on giving children a good place to play football, learn the game and enjoy being part of a team.

⚽

Player Development

Helping players improve their football skills, confidence and understanding of the game.

Player Development

Every player should have the opportunity to improve. We want our young footballers to develop skills that will stay with them throughout their football journey.

★

Quality Environment

A positive place where young players can enjoy football, meet people and develop their skills.

Quality Environment

Good facilities, equipment, coaching and organisation all contribute to giving our juniors an environment they can be proud to be part of.

↗

Future Pathway

Players can continue through youth football and eventually into senior football at Ngongotahā.

Future Pathway

Our long-term aim is to create a strong pathway from junior football through youth football and ultimately into our senior teams.
