Developing Young Players

Our Development Focus

At Ngongotahā AFC, our junior football programme is about helping players grow both on and off the pitch.

⚽

Football Development

Building strong football fundamentals while helping players develop their confidence, creativity and understanding of the game.

🤝

Teamwork

Teaching players how to work together, communicate, support their teammates and enjoy being part of a team.

🌱

Player Growth

Creating an environment where every player can develop at their own pace while being encouraged to keep learning.

😊

Enjoyment

Keeping football fun and positive so our young players build a lasting love for the game.

Football Rules

Game Formats

The game formats below remain unchanged. Teams move up a grade for the upcoming season, but the applicable football format remains tied to the relevant age-group rules.

First Kicks

6th & 7th Grade
3v3
Grades 6th and 7th Grade
Game Format 3v3
Goalkeeper No goalkeeper
Game Time 3 × 8 minute games
Pitch Size 20m × 15m
Goal Size Range: 1.5m × 0.9m to 2m × 1m
Ball Size Size 3
Tables & Results No recorded results or table keeping
Substitutions 0–1 rolling substitute
Retreating Line Halfway
Offsides No offsides
Penalties No penalties
Corners No corners
Equipment Minimum: covered shoes. Optimal: boots and shinpads.

Fun Football

8th & 9th Grade
5v5
Grades 8th and 9th Grade
Game Format 5v5
Goalkeeper Yes
Game Time 2 × 20 minute games
Pitch Size Optimal: 35m × 25m
Range: 30m × 20m to 40m × 30m
Penalty Area 15m × 6m
Goal Size Range: 3m × 2m to 4m × 2m
Ball Size 8th Grade: Size 3
9th Grade: Size 4
Tables & Results No results recorded. No table keeping.
Substitutions 2–3 rolling substitutions
Retreating Line Halfway
Offsides No offsides
Corners Yes
Equipment Boots and shinpads

Mini Football

10th & 11th Grade
7v7
Grades 10th and 11th Grade
Game Format 7v7
Goalkeeper Yes
Game Time 2 × 25 minute halves
Pitch Size Optimal: 50m × 30m
Range: 45m × 30m to 55m × 35m
Penalty Area 20m × 8m
Goal Size 4m × 2m
Ball Size Size 4
Tables & Results Results recorded for grading purposes only. No table keeping.
Substitutions 2–3 rolling substitutions
Retreating Line 30% of the field from the goal line
Offsides Yes, retreating line
Corners Yes
Equipment Boots and shinpads

Mini Football

12th Grade
9v9
Grade 12th Grade
Game Format 9v9
Goalkeeper Yes
Game Time 2 × 30 minute halves
Pitch Size Optimal: 70m × 50m
Range: 60m × 45m to 75m × 50m
Penalty Area 26m × 10m
Goal Size Optimal: 5m × 2m
Range: 5m × 2m to 6.4m × 2.2m
Ball Size Size 4
Tables & Results Results recorded for grading purposes only. No table keeping.
Substitutions 2–3 rolling substitutions
Retreating Line 30% of the field from the goal line
Offsides Yes, halfway
Penalties 8m
Free Kicks Direct or indirect. Opposition 5m away.
Corners Yes
Equipment Boots and shinpads
