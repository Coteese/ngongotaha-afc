Our Ambition

Ngongotahā AFC is serious about the future of junior football. We have big ambitions for our young players and for the future of the club.

Over recent years we have made huge progress in the development of our junior football programme. Our goal is to continue building an environment where young players can enjoy football, challenge themselves and develop the skills and confidence required to reach their potential.

With excellent youth facilities, dedicated coaches and a strong football community behind them, our junior players have the opportunity to develop in a positive and ambitious environment.

Our commitment is simple: we want Ngongotahā to be a club where parents feel confident their children are receiving the support, coaching and opportunities they need to grow as footballers and as people.
