Women's Football at Ngongotahā

This season, Ngongotahā AFC is taking another step forward with the introduction of a new women's football team. A new team. A new opportunity. A new chapter for our club.

A New Team. A New Opportunity.

Ngongotahā AFC has a proud football history in Rotorua, and now we are building the next part of that history.

01 • THE NEXT CHAPTER

Women's Football Is Here

We did not have a women's team last season. This season, that changes. We are creating an opportunity for women in Rotorua and the surrounding area to pull on the orange and black and become part of the Ngongotahā AFC family.

02 • BUILT FROM THE GROUND UP

Be Part of the Beginning

This is more than joining an existing team. It is an opportunity to help build something from the ground up, shape the culture of the team and create a women's programme that can grow for years to come.

What We Want to Build

The goal is simple: create a women's football environment where players want to turn up, compete, improve and be proud to represent Ngongotahā.

Build Something

A Club for the Whole Family

One of the things that makes a football club special is having a place for the whole family. Alongside the new women's team, Ngongotahā AFC also has a growing junior football programme.

That means players can be part of a club where their children can also enjoy football, meet friends and develop their own game. For mums, sisters, daughters and whānau, football can become something the whole family shares.

Whether you are coming back to football, looking for a new club or have never played senior football before, this is an opportunity to get involved.

Women's + Junior Football One club. Multiple generations. A football environment built around players, families and the wider community.
