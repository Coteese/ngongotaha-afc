Welcome to Ngongotahā Football Club

Ngongotahā AFC is Rotorua’s premier football club — proudly offering opportunities for players of all ages and abilities.

From our youngest Little Dribblers through to our junior, youth, and senior teams, everyone is welcome to play, learn, and grow with us here in Rotorua.

Our First Team competes in the LOTTO NRFL Southern Conference, the highest local football competition, providing talented players the chance to perform at an elite level while staying connected to their community roots.

At Ngongotahā AFC, we’re passionate about growing the beautiful game in Rotorua — whether you’re taking your first kick, returning to the pitch, or looking for a friendly, family-oriented club to call home.

Our Mission

We believe football is more than just a sport; it’s a way to bring people together, build lifelong friendships, and teach important values like teamwork, respect, and perseverance.
