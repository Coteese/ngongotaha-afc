NGONGOTAHĀ AFC

Built for Football

Ngongotahā Football Club is a community-focused football club providing opportunities for players of all ages and abilities.

From our youngest Little Dribblers through to our junior, youth and senior teams, our aim is to create an environment where players can enjoy football, develop their abilities and continue progressing through the club.
