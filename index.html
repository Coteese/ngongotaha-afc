THE CLUB

Welcome to Ngongotahā Football Club

Ngongotahā AFC is Rotorua's football club, proudly offering opportunities for players of all ages and abilities.

From our youngest Little Dribblers through to our junior, youth and senior teams, everyone is welcome to play, learn and grow with us here in Rotorua.

Our First Team competes in the LOTTO NRFL Southern Conference, providing talented players with the opportunity to compete at a high level while remaining connected to their community roots.

Whether you're taking your first kick, returning to the pitch, developing your game or looking for a club to call home, Ngongotahā Football Club is built around football, community and development.

CLUB HISTORY

60 Years of Ngongotahā AFC

Ngongotahā AFC is celebrating its 60th year jubilee in 2026, and what a 60 years it has been.

Founded in 1966, the club has grown from its early days into one of Rotorua's longstanding football institutions. Across six decades, Ngongotahā has been part of generations of local players' football journeys, from young children taking their first touches through to senior football.

The club's history includes a long spell in the Northern League from 1978 to 1994, with Ngongotahā reaching as high as the second division. The club also celebrated a Northern League Division Four South championship in 1983.

More recent milestones include the 2011 Waikato-Bay of Plenty Federation Championship, promotion to the Premiership, a Chatham Cup run to the round of 16 in 2014, and an unbeaten Federation Premiership title-winning campaign in 2016.

Today, the club continues to build a pathway from Little Dribblers and junior football through to women's and senior teams. The 60th year is a chance to celebrate the people, families, players, coaches, volunteers and supporters who have helped shape the club over six decades, while looking ahead to the next generation.

MAY 2026
COMMUNITY

Walking Football Launches in Rotorua

Ngongotahā AFC has expanded its football offering with the launch of Walking Football in partnership with Parksyde Community Centre.

The first session brought together members of Rotorua's over-55 community for a relaxed introduction to the game, combining gentle movement, football and social connection.

The programme is part of the club's wider focus on making football accessible to people of different ages and abilities, creating another way for the Rotorua community to stay active and connected through the game.

JAN 2026
SENIOR FOOTBALL

Preseason 2026 Gets Underway

The Ngongotahā AFC First Team and U23s began their preparations for the 2026 season in January.

The first preseason session was held at the club on Stembridge Road, bringing returning players and new faces together as the teams prepared for the season ahead.

With training building over the following weeks, the start of preseason marked another important step in developing the club's senior pathway and preparing for another season of football.
