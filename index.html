THE CLUB

Welcome to Ngongotahā Football Club

Ngongotahā AFC is Rotorua's football club, proudly offering opportunities for players of all ages and abilities.

From our youngest Little Dribblers through to our junior, youth and senior teams, everyone is welcome to play, learn and grow with us here in Rotorua.

Our First Team competes in the LOTTO NRFL Southern Conference, providing talented players with the opportunity to compete at a high level while remaining connected to their community roots.

Whether you're taking your first kick, returning to the pitch, developing your game or looking for a club to call home, Ngongotahā Football Club is built around football, community and development.

60 YEARS OF NGONGOTAHĀ AFC

A Club Built on Football, Community and Generations

Ngongotahā AFC is celebrating its 60th year jubilee in 2026, and what a 60 years it has been.

Founded in 1966, Ngongotahā Football Club has grown alongside the community it represents. What began as a local football club has developed into one of Rotorua's longstanding football institutions, providing a place for generations of players to learn the game, make friendships and pull on the Ngongotahā colours.

Over the decades, the club has competed across the regional and national football landscape. Ngongotahā spent many years competing in the Northern League era, including football as high as the second division. The club has also enjoyed memorable cup and championship campaigns, with the 2011 season bringing a Federation Championship title and promotion.

In 2014, Ngongotahā reached the round of 16 in the Chatham Cup, creating another memorable chapter in the club's history. Two years later came another milestone when the club claimed its first Waikato-Bay of Plenty Federation Premiership title, completing an unbeaten championship campaign.

But the story of Ngongotahā AFC is about much more than league tables and trophies. It is about the volunteers preparing the grounds, coaches giving their time, families standing on the sideline, juniors discovering football for the first time, and players returning to the club year after year.

As the club celebrates 60 years in 2026, the focus is both on remembering the people and moments that built Ngongotahā AFC and looking forward to the next generation. From Little Dribblers and junior football through to senior, women's and community football, the next chapter is already being written.

COMMUNITY FOOTBALL

Walking Football Launches in Rotorua

Ngongotahā AFC has helped launch Walking Football in Rotorua, opening another pathway for people to enjoy the game in a relaxed and welcoming environment.

The programme is designed for the over-55 community and offers a slower-paced version of football without running or contact. It provides an accessible way to stay active, meet people and continue enjoying the game regardless of previous football experience or current fitness level.

The programme is being led by Head Coach Marvin Koeppen, with sessions taking place in partnership with Parksyde Community Centre.

9 JAN
SENIOR FOOTBALL

Preseason 2026 Gets Underway

The First Team and U23s began preparations for the 2026 season with preseason training at Stembridge Road.

With another season ahead and plenty to build towards, the club welcomed new players to join the group and become part of the Ngongotahā senior football pathway.

The first session also included a club barbecue, bringing players, coaches and the wider football community together as the new season began.

30 JAN
COMMUNITY

Football Meets the Community at Fun Whānau Days

Ngongotahā AFC took football into the community during a series of Fun Whānau Days across Rotorua.

The club brought football stations, small goals, cones and plenty of footballs to local parks, giving tamariki and whānau the chance to get involved in a fun and informal setting.

The events are part of the club's continued commitment to making football accessible and welcoming for people throughout the Rotorua community.
