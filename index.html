THE CLUB

Welcome to Ngongotahā Football Club

Ngongotahā AFC is Rotorua's football club, proudly offering opportunities for players of all ages and abilities.

From our youngest Little Dribblers through to our junior, youth and senior teams, everyone is welcome to play, learn and grow with us here in Rotorua.

Our First Team competes in the LOTTO NRFL Southern Conference, providing talented players with the opportunity to compete at a high level while remaining connected to their community roots.

Whether you're taking your first kick, returning to the pitch, developing your game or looking for a club to call home, Ngongotahā Football Club is built around football, community and development.

OUR APPROACH

Our Mission

We believe football is more than just a sport. It is a way to bring people together, build lifelong friendships and develop important values such as teamwork, respect, discipline and perseverance.

Our aim is to create an environment where players can enjoy football, develop their abilities and continue progressing through the club at every stage of their football journey.
